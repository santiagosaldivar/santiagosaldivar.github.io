About

Hello! I am a Research Associate at OpenAI, based in Palo Alto, California. Previously, I was a Predoctoral Research Fellow in Economics at the Ellison Institute of Technology, where my research focused on the effects of AI on labor markets and health care, the impact of surgical robotics on patient outcomes and surgeon productivity, and heterogeneity in health and aging across demographic groups in the United Kingdom.

I graduated from Harvard College in 2024 with an A.B. in Applied Mathematics and Economics, where I completed an honors thesis studying social capital and high school graduation rates. Prior to joining EIT, I worked as a research assistant at the National Bureau of Economic Research and as a predoctoral fellow at Harvard Business School.

I was selected for the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program in 2026, and I plan to pursue a Ph.D. in Economics or a related field in the future.
